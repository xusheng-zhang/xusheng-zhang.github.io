About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Durham University, working with Peter Davies-Peck. Previously, I was a Senior Research Associate in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Oxford, working with Leslie Ann Goldberg. I received my PhD from Penn State in 2024, where I was advised by Antonio Blanca. Before graduate school, I built cloud products at Amazon Web Services.

Research

My research lies at the intersection of theoretical computer science, probability theory, and statistical physics. I am especially interested in sampling via Markov chains, distributed and decentralized algorithms, and stochastic processes on networks. I also enjoy mathematical and algorithmic puzzles.
